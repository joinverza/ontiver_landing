## Current direction

The latest visual reference is the live [Trustly business website](https://www.trustly.com/?r=0) and its [personal website](https://www.trustly.com/personal). This supersedes the preceding Payaza visual pass and the earlier Persona section-order direction. The similarly named Framer template in the historical research is a separate reference and is not the source for this pass.

Ontiver adapts the page composition, photography, navigation, spacing, and restrained interactions. Its green palette, Bricolage Grotesque, identity product content, individual/enterprise journeys, and evidence requirements remain its own. Trustly's payment copy, brand assets, customer logos, testimonials, performance numbers, certifications, and regulatory statements are not reused.

## What was actually inspected

The [sitemap index](https://www.trustly.com/sitemap.xml) links the [EU](https://www.trustly.com/site/eu/sitemap.xml) and [US](https://www.trustly.com/site/us/sitemap.xml) inventories. Together they yielded **2,247 distinct URLs**, including localized and regional variants. This is a URL inventory, not 2,247 browser reviews.

Actual browser research covered:

- The business and personal homepages, with section captures and navigation/motion inspection.
- **30 business URLs**, listed by template family below. All had desktop browser records; selected representative templates also had 390px mobile records.
- Personal, support, contact, payment lookup, complaint, legal, and blog templates; all four help categories and **29 linked help articles**, captured at desktop and mobile widths. Categories and articles share a small number of templates.
- The security page, including its split photograph/headline introduction and short security-feature presentation, plus the responsible-disclosure document layout.

These sets overlap: for example, blog and contact-sales appear in more than one research pass. Their counts should not be added as if they were distinct page totals. Localized legal variants and most individual news/press pages were inventoried, not separately visually reviewed.

## Visual system adapted to Ontiver

The reference's 1440px desktop content starts around x=103 and spans roughly 1,234px, with a 72px white navigation band. Most sections stay white, separated by whitespace and occasional pale or dark emphasis. Photography carries the layout through mosaics, tall feature images, large rounded cards and wide CTAs. Plain text does not need a box around every paragraph.

Ontiver's desktop navigation follows the full-width dropdown composition: a pale introduction column, bordered destination links and a photograph. Parent labels retain their own pages, with separate disclosure controls. A short hover-intent delay accommodates diagonal pointer travel; click, ArrowDown, Tab, Escape and outside-click dismissal remain available. Mobile keeps the native modal and expandable groups.

Measured reference headings were approximately 77px/62px for desktop hero/section titles and 42px/32px on a sampled mobile industry page. Ontiver intentionally follows its own larger shared scale: 48-96px hero titles, 40-64px section titles, 24-26px card titles, 18-20px subtitles, 18px body text and 14px metadata. `src/index.css` is the implementation source of truth. Bricolage Grotesque remains self-hosted with its OFL license; colors remain Ontiver's dark green `#002d0e`, primary `#009311`, link green `#007d21`, pale green and neutral white/gray surfaces.

## Motion: observed evidence versus adaptation

| Reference evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Ontiver treatment                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Product and industry records show text entrance triggers with no pin or scrub. Lines/characters enter their clipping region.                                                                                                              | CSS/IntersectionObserver reveals on text and photo panels; no GSAP-driven section translation or pinned storytelling.                                                                                                                                              |
| Personal-home runtime capture: 10 triggers, no recorded pin or scrub. Business home: 26 triggers, no pin, with two word-opacity scrub triggers. Azura, About, Careers and the sampled article each recorded one scrubbed heading trigger. | Selected statement text reveals its words progressively; ordinary content uses a simple entrance. This does not move or pin an entire section.                                                                                                                     |
| Personal walkthrough script advances every five seconds while visible on desktop, with hover/focus pauses and manual selection.                                                                                                           | The homepage workflow provides manual step buttons, visibility-aware five-second playback and an explicit play/pause control; reduced motion and the global motion pause stop automatic progression. The separate how-it-works page keeps all five steps readable. |
| Reference logo strips, explicit carousel controls and small color/hover transitions were observed.                                                                                                                                        | Ontiver uses its own content and approved records only; no Trustly logos or false partner strip. Controls remain keyboard accessible.                                                                                                                              |
| Photo parallax was not established by the reviewed runtime data or still captures.                                                                                                                                                        | The bounded vertical offset on the two privacy-story photographs is an Ontiver adaptation, not a measured Trustly behavior. Product, industry and security photo panels use reveals.                                                                               |
| Forms, legal bodies and document navigation keep stable layouts.                                                                                                                                                                          | Functional forms and long-form documents remain ordinary document interfaces. Native internal scrolling is preserved for menus and support conversations.                                                                                                          |

No inspected business/home trigger was pinned. The retired footer curtain, pinned solution sequence and scroll-driven use-case movement remain absent. Shared motion honors `prefers-reduced-motion`; the global pause exposes all reveal content and stops optional animation. Lenis is Ontiver's existing smooth-scroll implementation, not evidence of Trustly's scrolling library.

## Photography and proof

Pinterest was explicitly used for image discovery. Six new photos supplement the existing eight: `cityPortrait`, `studentLife`, `everydayPhone`, `smallBusiness`, `mobileDetail` and `cityArchitecture`. Each was traced from an actual Pinterest pin to the original Pexels photographer page. The six additions total 1,469,208 bytes as optimized local WebP files. Their pin links, redirect/canonical details, original download URLs, photographers, dimensions, file sizes and [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/) are recorded in [image-sources.md](image-sources.md).

The app serves `public/assets/photos/` files through the `src/data/imagery.ts` registry, with alternative text, dimensions and focal positions. The audit found all 14 files registered, documented and referenced by app source, including all six additions in the feature imagery. There are no unused or unregistered photos in that directory. Pinterest previews are not used as production assets. People pictured are editorial subjects, not Ontiver staff, customers or endorsers. The Zurich streetscape does not assert service availability in Switzerland.

The original evidence rules remain in force: approved records are required before partner, recognition or certification logos can render; the three reserved pilot metrics remain pending; proposed pilots, forecasts and architecture are not published as achieved commercial proof. No reference statistic or certification is transferable to Ontiver. Detailed rules remain in [redesign-reference-notes.md](redesign-reference-notes.md).

## Validation boundary

The completed app review covered all 43 routes at 1440px and 390px, plus selected 320px layouts. Checks found one H1 per page, consistent loaded Bricolage typography, no horizontal overflow, no clipped text and no browser errors. Page-family reviews also checked photographs, retained content and destination links.

Interaction checks covered desktop dropdowns for both audiences at 1250px and 1440px, mobile menu accordions and scroll locking, keyboard and native-touch carousel navigation, pricing switches and calculator behavior, workflow playback and pause conditions, the shared animation pause across routes, and live reduced-motion changes. Contact, enterprise contact, support, waitlist, newsletter and deletion forms were exercised with mocked requests; no real form submissions were sent.

`npm run lint` and `npm run build` passed. The production build prerendered all 43 routes. Vite reports the existing large-chunk warning for the 636.73 kB JavaScript bundle (189.21 kB gzip); this is a warning, not a build failure. The work remains local and is not deployed. These checks do not claim that every enumerated Trustly URL or every live production integration was tested.
